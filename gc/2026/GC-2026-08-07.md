## 2. Open Discussions & Decisions

- **Topic:** Payments Tech Council (PTC)
  - **Notes:** Received 29 nominations for the Payments Tech Council. GC discussed an objective criteria to drive the qualifications, which included: Industry representation (ecosystem), bringing a distinct value to the protocol (expertise), having direct application of UCP (relevance), and history of prior contributions (contributions). GC discusses the PTC could quickly stand up one or more specific DWGs to increase the representation and leverage the expertise of the applicants.
  - **Agreement:** GC will grade the candidates based on this criteria and recommend a seat assignment proposal for the next GC meeting.
  - **Context:** [Inviting nominations for UCP Payments Tech Council](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/624)

- **Topic:** Feeds Domain Working Group (DWG)
  - **Notes:** Discussed the proposal to create a domain working group to focus on feeds and bulk product discovery.
  - **Agreement:** GC Approved the proposal.
  - **Context:** [Domain Working Group (DWG) Charter: Bulk Product Discovery](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/issues/682)
